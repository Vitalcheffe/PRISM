05 Cascade Above cascade threshold, secondary collapses. Excess amplifies quadratically: intensity + excess · k · (1 + excess · 0.1).
06 Exponential Runaway Self-reinforcing inflation above 8%. Wage-price spiral: min(1, exp(diff · steepness) − 1) saturates at 100%.
07 Thermodynamic Equilibrium Diminishing returns + system tension. Michaelis-Menten saturates; Σ sector fitness is conserved. Over-optimizing one penalizes the whole.
